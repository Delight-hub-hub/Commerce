Collaborated with my group to create an e-commerce-store
It is built using React, Tailwind CSS, React Router for navigation, and Redux for state management.
It has 6 pages, which are the Home page, Product Detail, Cart , Checkout, Address Form, and Card Form.
Each of these pages display different sections
On the Homepage, you will find the sidebar, which navigates to different pages, the search bar, the product grid, which displays the different products with their names and prices and the cartsummmary, with the product items you would have selected
On the The Product Detail, each product that you select from the product grid, you get to find out more about it in detail, and decide whether you will add it on your cart or not.
The Cart page shows the different products you selected and you can add more and be seeing your total price on the right.
Then the button for checkout on the right of your cart page leads you the checkout page where you will have two buttons that say "change" which when you click, one will lead you to the add address form and the other to the add card form
